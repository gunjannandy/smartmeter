# After a power cut, Sockets stay off until the Driver confirms

An automatic resume would bill whoever is plugged in when power returns, which may not be the Driver who started the Session. Instead, the Box comes back with all Sockets off, the Driver is notified that charging stopped, and one tap resumes it. Anything charged after the tap is billed to that Driver, and the Host can see the same history. We accept that an unattended overnight charge may stay unfinished.
