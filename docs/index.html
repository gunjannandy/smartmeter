Keshtopur Toto Stand

This preview needs WebGL, which this browser does not offer. The idea in short:

  1. A driver pulls up and scans the QR on a plug-point unit.
  2. The Box meters that plug point on its own and stops when the battery is full.
  3. A power cut pauses every session. After power returns, each driver taps to resume.
  4. The Host sets the price per unit and keeps the accounts. The software never touches the money.
